# ADR 0009: Pause the personal decision-product direction

- **Effective date:** 2026-09-21
- **Status:** Superseded in part by ADR 0010
- **Recorded:** 2026-10-22, retrospectively from the implementation history

## Context

The implemented evidence engine and manual workflows had not established enough recurring value to justify continuing the personal career-decision product in its existing form.

## Decision

Pause that development direction and retain the working evidence engine and tested contracts. Do not interpret completed infrastructure as proof of product usefulness.

## Consequences

The project retained reusable collection and reporting components. A later resumption needed a new product question and an explicit scope decision.
