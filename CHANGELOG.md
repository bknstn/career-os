# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — UI 2.0

### Changed

- Focused the Radar overview on exploring job evidence, with one primary action,
  two summary counts, a compact role list, and two reviewed research highlights.
- Moved source status, historical comparisons, and research context into
  expandable details while keeping data coverage warnings visible.
- Improved typography, spacing, contrast, and mobile navigation; removed the
  animated radar decoration, blinking cursor, and background grid.
- Made research cards clickable across their surface and retained direct access
  to unclassified postings.

The application version advances from 0.1.0 to 1.0.0. UI 2.0 names this interface
redesign; it is separate from the application release number.
